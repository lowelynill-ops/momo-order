1. Who we are

  • You place an order by choosing items, entering your name and phone number, and paying online or in cash at the counter.
  • An order is accepted only when our team accepts it. We may refuse or cancel an order, for example if an item has run out, the details are wrong, or we are too busy.
  • Please check your items and total before you pay. Once cooking has started we may not be able to change or cancel it.

5. Payment

  • Online (UPI): You can pay through a UPI app on your phone or by scanning the payment QR code. The payment is made between you, your UPI app and your bank. We do not collect or see your card, bank or UPI PIN details.

  • We are not responsible for failed, delayed or declined payments caused by your bank, UPI app, network or phone. If money is deducted but your order is not confirmed, please show your payment screen and your order token to our staff at the counter.
  • Please keep your payment confirmation until you have received your order.

6. Cancellations and refunds

  • You can ask to cancel only before cooking starts. Please speak to our staff at the counter.
  • If we cancel an order or cannot serve an item you have already paid for, we will refund the amount for that item to the account you paid from, as soon as we reasonably can.
  • We do not give refunds for food that was prepared correctly as ordered, or for a change of mind after cooking has started.

7. Preparation time

Any preparation time we show or tell you is only an estimate. It can change when the kitchen is busy.

8. Allergies and dietary needs

Our food is made in a kitchen that handles ingredients such as wheat (gluten), soy, dairy, eggs, nuts, sesame and fish. We cannot guarantee that any item is free from these. If you have an allergy or special dietary need, please tell our staff before ordering. Notes written on this page may not be seen in time, so please do not rely on them for allergies.

9. Item photos and descriptions

Photos are for illustration. The actual dish, portion and spice level may look or taste a little different.

10. Using this page properly

Please do not place fake or repeated orders, enter other people's details without permission, or try to interfere with or misuse this page or our systems. We may block orders that look like misuse.

Privacy Policy

Momo Mama, Kolkata. Last updated: October 2026.

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this ordering page, why we collect it, and what we do with it. By ticking the box and placing an order, you agree to it.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name and phone number, your table number, the items you order, any notes you write, and the order total.
  • Payment information: whether you chose cash or UPI, which UPI app or QR you used, and whether the order is marked paid. We do not collect or store your card numbers, bank account details or UPI PIN. Your UPI payment is handled by your own UPI app and bank.
  • Information saved in your browser: your cart and, while a payment is being confirmed, a reference to your order, so the page can carry on if you switch apps. This stays on your phone.

2. Why we use it

  • To prepare and serve your order and to tell you when it is placed or ready to collect.
  • To confirm your payment, give you a bill, and keep records for accounting and tax.
  • To contact you about a problem with your order, such as a payment or item issue.
  • To prevent fake orders and misuse, and to improve our menu and service.

We do not use your phone number for advertising or sell it to anyone.

3. Who can see it

  • Our staff, to handle your order.
  • Our technology provider, Google (Firebase), which stores order data for us on its servers.

4. Third-party content on this page

This page loads fonts from Google Fonts and some food photos from other websites. Those services can see your device's IP address and basic browser details when the page loads. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to run the business, handle complaints, and meet accounting and tax rules. After that we delete them or remove the personal details.

6. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting who in our team can see orders. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, or delete them, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Please ask our staff at the counter. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it, and if you withdraw consent we may not be able to take your order.
